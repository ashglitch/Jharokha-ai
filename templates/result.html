READ THIS BEFORE INTERPRETING

A screening aid,
not a safety verdict.

The score, tier and interval come from simple rules on crack width, density and branching. They do not know the material, load, foundation or moisture, so they cannot tell you how many years a structure has left. Carvings, jali, joints and shadows can still be mistaken for cracks. Check the overlay against the photo and ask a conservation or structural professional about anything suspected.
